Tabata & HIIT Timer

Privacy Policy

No data collected

Summary: Tabata & HIIT Timer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or non-personal data. Everything stays on your device. The app works fully offline, has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app does not ask for or access any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or payment details
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Health, fitness, or workout-adjacent health records
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics
  • Anything else that could identify you

There is no account, no sign-up, and no login — so there is no personal data held by us, on our servers or anywhere else.

2. How your data is stored

All app content is stored locally, inside the app's own sandbox on your device:

  • Your settings and preferences (for example, theme choice) are saved with UserDefaults on your device.
  • Your custom workouts and presets are saved as a local JSON file in the app's private Application Support directory.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all of it.

3. Network and data transmission

The app makes no internet connections at all. It contains no code that uploads, downloads, synchronises, or backs up your data. It can be used in Airplane Mode with no loss of functionality.

4. Advertising, analytics and tracking

Tabata & HIIT Timer is free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

  • No advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No cookies, pixels, or fingerprinting of any kind
  • No third-party SDKs whatsoever

5. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not have any data to share. The app does not link to, embed, or integrate any third-party content, social features, or payment systems.

6. Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for general audiences and is directed at a general (12+) audience, in line with App Store guidelines.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains functionality that changes this policy, this page will be updated before or at the same time as that change is released. The "last updated" date below shows the current version of this policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us via the developer's GitHub profile or open an issue in the project repository.
